Your 8-week weight loss plan
Designed for an intermediate runner at 13.5 stone, targeting ~1 stone of fat loss before your end-of-July trip. Four sessions per week, 30–60 minutes each, built around your existing 2–3 runs per week.

The three phases
Phase 1 — Build
Weeks 1–3
1 June – 21 June
Establish aerobic base. Shorter sessions, manageable intensity. The goal is consistency — all four sessions completed each week is the win.
Phase 2 — Develop
Weeks 4–6
22 June – 12 July
Ramp up calorie burn. Longer sessions, higher intensity intervals, fasted cardio introduced. Week 6 is a deliberate deload to let the body adapt.
Phase 3 — Peak & taper
Weeks 7–8
13 July – 26 July
Peak effort in Week 7 — longest run, most intervals, longest tempo. Week 8 tapers back down so you arrive at your trip energised and looking your best.

⚠
Knee & lower back guidance
Weak right knee: Avoid deep lunges and steep downhill running. Keep cycling cadence high (80–90 rpm) — this reduces knee load. On bad knee days, swap any run for an equivalent bike session at the same intensity structure. Stop if the knee swells.

Occasional lower back pain: The core work in active recovery sessions (dead bugs, glute bridges, planks) is therapeutic, not filler — prioritise it. On flare-up days, skip sit-up style movements entirely. Avoid locked-knee cycling. Never rush a warm-up.
How to progress each week
↗
The 10% rule
Never increase total weekly volume by more than 10% in one go. The plan is already built to respect this — don't be tempted to add extra sessions in the early weeks.
⚠
Honour the deload
Week 6 feels easy. That's the point. Skipping the deload week is the fastest route to injury or burnout just before the peak phase.
⇆
Swap days freely
Sessions are labelled A/B/C/D not by day. The only rule: don't put two hard sessions back-to-back. Keep at least one easy day between intervals and tempo.
📈
Listen to RPE
Rate of Perceived Exertion matters more than pace or speed. Easy = conversational (5/10). Tempo = comfortably hard (7/10). Intervals = 8–9/10. Adjust for heat, tiredness, or life stress.

Protein is the priority
Eat 130–150g protein per day. It preserves muscle while losing fat, kills hunger, and keeps metabolism higher. Include a protein source at every meal — eggs, chicken, Greek yogurt, fish, cottage cheese, legumes.
⚖
Carb cycle around sessions
Eat more carbs on workout days to fuel sessions and recovery. On rest days, cut rice, pasta, and bread portions by about a third and replace with extra vegetables. You don't need to count grams — just adjust portions.
💧
Hydration
2.5–3 litres of water daily. 500ml before breakfast, 500ml during sessions over 45 min. Add a pinch of salt and diluted juice as electrolytes for longer sessions. Dehydration mimics hunger and tanks performance.
⏰
Meal timing
Eat your largest meal within 60 minutes post-workout. Have a small carb snack 30 minutes before harder sessions. On fasted session days (Week 4+), train before breakfast then eat within 30 minutes of finishing.
💤
Sleep — the hidden tool
7–8 hours per night. Poor sleep raises hunger hormones by up to 24% and wrecks recovery. Prioritise sleep as much as the sessions themselves.
📈
Track weekly, not daily
Weigh yourself at the same time, same day, each week (e.g. Wednesday morning). Daily fluctuations of 1–2 lbs are completely normal. The trend over 4+ weeks is what matters.
Do and don't
✓ Do
✓Warm up for 5 minutes before every session
✓Keep easy sessions genuinely easy — zone 2, conversational pace
✓Swap a run for the bike on bad knee days
✓Drink water before, during, and after every session
✓Eat protein within 60 minutes post-workout
✓Take the Week 6 deload seriously
✓Move sessions around the week to fit your schedule
✓Do core work (dead bugs, glute bridges) to protect the lower back
✗ Don't
✗Run two hard sessions back-to-back
✗Skip the warm-up when short on time
✗Push through sharp knee pain — rest or bike instead
✗Add extra sessions in early weeks "because it feels easy"
✗Eat in a large deficit on heavy session days
✗Run steep downhills at pace — walk them if knee is sore
✗Rely on the scale daily — it will lie to you
✗Underestimate alcohol — 2 pints is ~400 kcal
